SERVICES

Scheduling support across the life of the project

Every service below is scoped for high-voltage transmission, substation, and utility-scale renewable interconnection work — not adapted from a generic commercial-construction template.

SCH-CPM

CPM Schedule Development

Baseline P6 schedules for HV transmission, substations, and MV overhead line construction — including Hendrix spacer cable systems and bare wire construction — built from real construction logic instead of a recycled activity library.

SCH-BID

Bid & Proposal Schedule Support

Schedule of values, milestone schedules, and P6 submission schedules built to align with your estimate package and survive owner scrutiny.

SCH-UPD

Progress Updates & Reporting

Periodic status updates, progress curves, and schedule narratives that keep the PM, the client, and the field looking at the same picture.

SCH-RSK

Sequencing & Constructability Review

Logic and sequencing risk flagged before it becomes a field problem — long-lead procurement, permit gating, circuit and crew sequencing.

SCH-FOR

Delay & Forensic Analysis

Time impact analysis, as-built reconciliation, and schedule documentation to support delay claims and recovery planning.

SCH-SET

P6 Environment Setup

EPS/WBS structure, calendars, and activity coding standards for teams standing up or cleaning up their own P6 environment.

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps, one point of contact

No account team, no bench of junior schedulers. You work directly with the person building the schedule, start to finish.

01 · INTAKE

Scope the schedule

Contract documents, RFP requirements, and estimate assumptions reviewed to define schedule basis, milestones, and level of detail.

02 · BUILD

Build in P6

Activities, logic, durations, and calendars developed in Primavera P6 Professional, coded to a consistent activity structure.

03 · REVIEW

Pressure-test it

Critical path, float, and sequencing reviewed against real constructability — not just logic-tied for the sake of a clean network.

04 · DELIVER

Hand it off

Final schedule, narrative, and supporting workbooks delivered in the format your team and your owner expect.
